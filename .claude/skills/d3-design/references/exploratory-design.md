# Exploratory design

Absorbed from the v2 `exploratory-design` skill. The whole of it now lives in the "Before code" section of `SKILL.md`; this page keeps the original framing for anyone who opens it directly.

The viewer is a detective, not a reader. Open a thinking space instead of guiding them to an answer.

1. **See the data.** Profile shape and structure before choosing a form: dimensions, distributions, clusters, gaps. Hierarchical, relational, temporal, spatial, or high-dimensional?
2. **Define the task.** What does the viewer *do*, not see? Discover, compare, relate. Exploration before explanation.
3. **Find the natural form.** Match the form to the data's structure; don't flatten what's naturally complex. Interaction is integral to the form, not added after: every interaction answers a question the viewer might ask. Coordinate views when one can't hold the full structure, so understanding comes from how the views respond together.

Complexity matches the data. Sub-100ms response means the viewer is thinking with the tool. Animation only for object constancy.
