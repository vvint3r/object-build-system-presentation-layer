## Ruling

Diagrams, charts and visualizations get a build of their own, structured from a reference set the
owner supplies, which includes a taxonomy that may or may not serve as its outline. The library's
diagram and chart pages and the visualization kit (its Obsidian charts and diagrams) are references
for that build, not sources adopted as they are. It is the next migration step after the decision
framework, and work on it starts when the owner shares the reference set.
