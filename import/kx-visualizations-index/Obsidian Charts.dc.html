REFTokens, palettes and the shared chart spec — read this first
Assumptions I made
Everything on this page is a live ECharts 5.5.1 render using the SVG renderer — the same library, renderer and font stack as the vault, so every value in the spec strips is provable, not interpreted. Panels are drawn at their real pixel widths: 730px (column), 900px (bleed) and 349px (half-column pair). Data is generic business (revenue, users, channels, segments).
Two findings worth knowing before you wire anything: ECharts has no letter-spacing option in any textStyle — the 1.2px token can only be applied with a CSS rule on the SVG (.chart svg text{letter-spacing:.5px}), which is why SVG renderer matters. And Nunito 300 fails at tick-label sizes on #171717 — the stems thin out to under a device pixel and fringe. Tick labels below 13px move to weight 500, and every number moves to Fira Code 400.

#25d09d #ff7d8a #6ea8ff
#ffca00 #b191ff #3fb6c9
Your six, with two changes. Coral moves to slot 2 so a two-series chart gets maximum hue separation instead of teal-next-to-blue. Cyan drops from #46d4e0 to #3fb6c9 — the original sat 8° from teal at nearly the same lightness and collapsed under deuteranopia.
Safe to 4 series for protan/deutan; 6 for tritan. All ≥4.5:1 on #171717.
B · Voltage
#00da8e #f8007c #ffdf00
#00b4ff #a56bff #4bf07a
Built from the #f8007c / #00ff1a direction. Reads loud and looks great as strokes. I substituted #4bf07a for #00ff1a — pure green at full chroma halates on OLED and its 1px strokes bloom into unreadable fuzz.
Cap at 4 series. #00da8e and #4bf07a merge for deutans — never adjacent. Use for strokes and marks, not large fills.
C · Okabe-Ito, dark-lifted
#17b58a #56b4e9 #eda51a
#dd8ab6 #8f9bff #ece463
The Okabe-Ito set, lightness-lifted for a #171717 ground (#0072B2 blue was too dark to read and became #8f9bff). Use when a chart carries six or more series, or when the note goes to someone else.
Distinguishable at 6 series under all three deficiencies. Off-token — teal is no longer #25d09d.
Sequential ramp — for magnitude (heatmap, treemap, single-hue bar)
#12241f #173a30 #195244 #1a6c57 #1a886c #1ea683 #25d09d #6ee2c0 #b4f0dc
Nine OKLCH-even steps through the accent hue. Step 1 is only 4% lighter than the panel, so the low end still reads as “present but empty” rather than as a hole. Feed to visualMap.inRange.color.
Diverging ramp — for signed change (variance, delta, sentiment)
#ff9aa4 #e0707d #a8515c #6b3a41 #2e2e2e #1f5348 #1a8a6c #25d09d #8ae8c9
Coral↔teal through a neutral #2e2e2e midpoint rather than through white — on a dark ground a light midpoint becomes the loudest thing on the chart, which inverts the meaning of “no change”.
