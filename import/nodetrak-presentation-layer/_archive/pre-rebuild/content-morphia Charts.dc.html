Charts

A chart in this system answers one question and states its scale. Four shapes cover everything the index needs to quantify — magnitude, trend, spread, share — and a fifth state covers the case where the data is not there to draw.

Spread and share

Two charts that report a population rather than a series. A distribution shows where the mass sits and marks its median; a share bar divides one stated total. Neither may be replaced by an average, and neither is ever drawn as a circle.
