Structure & notation

Four shapes of argument the earlier waves could not make: a subject decomposed outward, membership across overlapping sets, many causes converging on one effect, and a process written in the notation engineers already read. Capacity rules come from Diagrams at scale; depth and fill come from R12.4 / R12.5.

Mind map

T2 · 14 nodes · 3 rings
Decomposition, not flow. Edges carry no direction and no label — the only claim is that a child belongs to its parent. Branches balance left and right of the subject so the whole map reads at one glance, and every label stays horizontal: rotated text is what makes most mind maps unreadable.
