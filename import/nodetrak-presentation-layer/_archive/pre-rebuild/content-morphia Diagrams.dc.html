Diagram library

One grammar, many diagram types. Nodes are white and softly lifted, markers are black-bordered, the accent never speaks: it points. Every diagram ships with a text alternative and scrolls sideways on small screens instead of shrinking.

Behavior

Collapsed groups keep their mono item count visible.
The selected node is a black-bordered pill with the accent chip, one per tree.
Long labels truncate with an ellipsis and keep their full text on hover.
Deep structures indent 26px per level; dotted guides mark the parent line.

Maturity model

Tier 1 · 5 stages
Stages ascend left to right: teal = achieved, black = current, dashed outline = target. Sample: metric governance maturity.
1 · Ad hoc
Metrics live in dashboards; no owners.
2 · Repeatable
Definitions written down; ad-hoc review.
CURRENT
3 · Defined
Registry live; owners and formulas required.
TARGET · Q4
4 · Managed
SLOs enforced; freshness + quality paired.
5 · Optimizing
Metrics drive automated remediation.
GuidanceAdvance one stage at a time: to reach Managed, certify the top-20 metrics and turn tier targets into enforced SLOs.
