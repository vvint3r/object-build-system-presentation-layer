Certification as a merge gate

One domain turned certification from an advisory check into a blocking one over six months. This node records what was measured before and after, what else changed in the same period, and which parts of it were withdrawn.
Case study Payments domain Published 34 tables · 9 teams · Feb–Jul 2026
ss: 01

What this is evidence for

A proof node exists to support a claim made somewhere else. The claim is stated first, in the words the method uses, so a reader can tell within one sentence whether this instance is relevant to them.

The claim under test

A blocking certification gate reduces definition drift without materially slowing delivery.
Half held. Drift incidents fell from fourteen a quarter to three, and the share of certified tables rose from eleven of thirty-four to thirty-one. Delivery did slow: the median schema change took half a day longer at the end of the period than at the start.
Evidence for Pipeline certification rollout, which cites this node at its gating decision.

Before and after

Four measures, each read the same way in both periods, each carrying its own value beside the bar. The measure that moved the wrong way sits in the same list as the three that improved: a set of results with no losses in it gets discounted whole, and rightly.
Two of the four had something else change underneath them inside the after period. Annotating them is not a caveat on the finding; it is what lets a reader decide how much of the movement to attribute to the gate.

Limitation
