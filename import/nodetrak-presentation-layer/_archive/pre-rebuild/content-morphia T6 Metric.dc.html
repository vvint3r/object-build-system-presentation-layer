Definition & formula

A table is fresh when its most recent successful load is younger than its declared SLA. The metric is the share of tracked tables passing that check. Coverage is tracked separately as its own metric: untracked tables can never flatter this number.
freshness_pct = 100 × fresh_tables / sla_tracked_tables
fresh_table ⇔ now() − max(loaded_at) ≤ sla_hours(table)
Note
Batch warehouse tables only. Streaming topics are excluded: ingestion lag (p95) guards those. Computed hourly; the reported value is the 09:00 UTC snapshot.
