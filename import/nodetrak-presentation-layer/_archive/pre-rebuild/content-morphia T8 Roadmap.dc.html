Analytics platform — FY27 roadmap

Three tracks across four quarters: unify instrumentation, enforce warehouse contracts, open self-serve activation. Reviewed monthly at the data council.
Roadmap Analytics platform In review Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 · owner: data platform
Objective Timeline Next milestone All milestones Risks

Objective

FY26 ended with four tracking SDKs, contract-less Tier-1 tables, and activation gated on the platform team. FY27 closes those three gaps in order — instrumentation first, because everything downstream inherits its quality.
FY27 exit criteria
One governed event stream — schema v1 everywhere, legacy tracker off
Contracts with paging SLAs on every Tier-1 table
Three teams shipping on self-serve activation
