Eight containers to build in, and for every shipped component the parts it is made of, the field each part reads, and whether that field is required, optional or computed. Selection says which component the material calls for; this says what the component needs before it can be rendered at all.
Every surface in this system is one of eight containers. They are a closed set: material that fits none of them is prose, and prose sits in the reading column without a container around it. The geometry column is the whole specification, a primitive has no variants and no size options.

Each part of a component belongs to one of three classes, and the class decides what happens when the field behind it is absent. Three marks carry it, and they are the only three in the register.
Req
Required
The component cannot render without it. A missing required field is not an empty part: it disqualifies the component, and the material takes a simpler one.
Opt
Optional
Renders when the field carries data and vanishes entirely when it does not. No empty frame, no dash, no line reading not available.
Derived
Computed
Produced by the renderer from other parts: step numbers, staleness, set size, zebra, confidence. Supplied by the model, it is ignored.
