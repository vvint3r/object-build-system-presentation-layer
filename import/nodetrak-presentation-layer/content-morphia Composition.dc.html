Mono is meta, never a heading
JetBrains Mono at 9.5–10px carries units, identifiers, timestamps, table headers, chip text and provenance. It may never be a container's only heading: a container's title is T3. A bulleted item's explanatory sub-line is body text at 13.5px, never mono.
A part transition outranks what it contains
A pillar or part boundary is T2 on its own full-width surface, with the pillar name and section range in mono beneath and 96px of clearance above. It is never a chip, and never smaller than the section titles inside it.
Emphasis is rationed, not available. Every device below carries a per-view limit, because the failure mode of this system is many correct components competing in one screen.
Semantic labels have two intensities
Tint is the default and carries no border. Solid is a verdict — the one status in a view that must stop the reader — and takes a 1.5px black border. At most one solid label per view. A semantic label is never a heading and never a table column header.

The page is the default surface. A container is a claim that its contents are separable from the flow, so it has to earn the enclosure.
Wrap only when one of these is true
{{w}}
Sequential prose, numbered lists and single explanatory passages sit directly on the page. Never two container levels; a container is never another container's first child.
Heading, prose, then containers
A group of containers that directly follows a heading is preceded by at least one sentence naming what the group is and why these items are grouped. A heading is never followed immediately by cards. A group that follows prose or another container already has its context and needs no lead.
Dividers separate siblings only
A hairline or dotted rule is legal only between two sibling sections sharing one surface, inset to the content padding. Never between a heading and its own body, never between list items, never directly above or below a container. Spacing does that work.
Depth follows position, not preference
Rule 5 gives five recipes; this decides which one. Two combinations are illegal in every case: a blurred shadow with a border, and two S2 peers side by side.

One display slab per section
The black slab is reserved for a section's defining equation: one per section, interior padding ≥24px. Every other expression is inline.

Inline math is not a chip
Raw arithmetic inside a sentence is plain mono text and takes no fill: 0.01 × 60,000 = 600. A chip means the token is named again elsewhere, and it is mono on white with a 1px #ebebeb hairline at radius 6 — δ_achieved — never a black fill.
A link carries one marker
The teal underline is the marker: 1.5px thickness, 3px offset. An inline link takes nothing else. The arrow prefix belongs to standalone jump lists, where the arrow replaces a bullet, and never appears on a link inside a sentence.
A figure that does not say what it is becomes decoration. Chrome that is not specified gets the browser's default, which belongs to no design system.

{{f}}
Magnitude bars are 6px tall on a #f1f1f1 track with the row label at 700. No chip sits inside a bar row; provenance goes below the figure.
Diagrams use three layouts, and nothing else
Nodes size to their text; arrows run on the layout's own axes. No absolute positioning, no crossing connectors, no node narrower than its longest word. Material that fits none of the three becomes a table.

Scrollbars
8px, black thumb at radius 999, #f1f1f1 track. Declared once in the helmet reset; never left to the browser default on a scrolling table or rail.
The sticky rail
Maximum 260px wide and 60vh tall, scrolling internally past that, and collapsing to a top strip at 1150px per rule 12. A rail never occupies more than a quarter of the viewport's width or more than half its height.
