Four additions to the diagram grammar.
R11.1
Lane rail
Every lane diagram carries a fixed 152px mono rail on the left. Lane names live in the rail and never inside the first content column, so column one stays comparable with the rest.
R11.2
Proportional magnitude
Magnitude flow is the one family exempt from the three-step edge-weight rule. Band width is continuously proportional on a single scale across all columns, and the scale plus the column total are always stated in the legend.
R11.3
Boundary lines are labelled
Blueprint lines are the subject, not decoration: solid black for interaction, dashed black for visibility, dotted for internal interaction. Each carries a mono label sitting on the line. No boundary line is ever drawn unlabelled.
R11.4
Sentiment is position
Emotion is encoded by vertical position on a labelled three-tick axis, plus a mood word in mono under each phase. Never by colour, never by face glyphs.
