The floor became the default

Tile gaps have a floor of 16. The build used gap:16 one hundred and eighty-four times — more than every other gap value combined. Module padding has a floor of 24, and 24 outnumbers the 26 and 28 the spec prefers for primary containers by two to one. Told a minimum and nothing else, an implementer builds at the minimum everywhere, and the result is a page with one spacing value.

How to read it

Find the pair, use the number. If a pair is not listed, it is not an adjacency this system produces — which usually means the composition is wrong, not that the table is incomplete. Nothing here is a suggestion and nothing here is a range.

What happened to the floors

They stay, demoted to what they always were: a check that catches a violation after the fact. Six of the values above sit exactly on their floor and are marked. The rest sit above it, which is the point — a floor was never meant to be the answer.
Rule 9 states a scale of 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 96. The agent build matched the pages rather than the spec, which was the right instinct. The spec was what was wrong.

Proposed replacement — two scales, by altitude

Structural · 4px steps
16 · 20 · 24 · 28 · 32 · 48 · 88 · 96
Anything at module altitude or above: section rhythm, module padding, gaps between surfaces, column gaps. The 4px discipline holds here and always has.
Component · sub-4px steps to 14
2 · 3 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 9 · 10 · 12 · 14
Interior chrome: chip and pill padding, label offsets, table cell padding, icon gaps. A 4px grid cannot express a 9.5px mono label sitting correctly inside a pill, which is why every page quietly abandoned it.
This is a description of what seventeen pages already do, not a new direction. One exception is sanctioned rather than hidden: 26px primary-container padding (27 uses) sits off the 4px structural steps and stays, because rule 5 gives primary containers a visibly deeper inset than their siblings. It stops the spec from lying without touching a single page.

The one exemption

A catalogue page — Foundations, Diagrams, this one — exists to show specimens side by side, so its density is its subject. It declares the exemption in its header and nothing else may claim it. A document that is teaching something is never a catalogue.
Read the shape of the source material, never its topic. A page about a process does not get a process diagram; material that is ordered steps does. This is the mapping the last build had to guess at, and guessing by subject is what produced nineteen tables.
