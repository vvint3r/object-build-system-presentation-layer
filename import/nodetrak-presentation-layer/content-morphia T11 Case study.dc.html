A proof node exists to support a claim made somewhere else. The claim is stated first, in the words the method uses, so a reader can tell within one sentence whether this instance is relevant to them.

The claim under test

A blocking certification gate reduces definition drift without materially slowing delivery.
Half held. Drift incidents fell from fourteen a quarter to three, and the share of certified tables rose from eleven of thirty-four to thirty-one. Delivery did slow: the median schema change took half a day longer at the end of the period than at the start.
