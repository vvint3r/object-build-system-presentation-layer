Billing ticket escalation to specialists

How a Tier 1 agent hands a billing ticket to the billing specialists and follows it to resolution.

Part 01

Purpose

Tier 1 agents escalate a billing ticket when it needs a decision only the billing specialists can make. Large refunds, disputed charges and invoice corrections are the usual cases. The agent who escalates stays on the ticket until it closes.

Part 02

Prerequisites

An agent needs three things in place before the first step.

  • The Tier 1 role in the billing console, which allows refunds and credits of up to $250 per invoice.S1
  • The Billing v4 macro set in the helpdesk, which holds the Escalate to billing macro.S2
  • The customer's identity, confirmed through the account checks in the helpdesk sidebar.
Part 03

Steps

An escalation runs in seven steps, from the first check to the closed ticket. The Tier 1 agent stays on the ticket as a follower after the hand-off.

  1. Set the billing intent

    Set the ticket's intent field to Billing if routing has not set it. Chat arrives classified; email, phone, web form and social tickets need the field set by hand.

    Outcome

    The ticket sits in the billing view with its intent set.

  2. Check whether Tier 1 can resolve it

    Tier 1 resolves invoice explanations, payment method changes, and refunds or credits within the agent limit. For any of these, resolve the ticket directly and skip the remaining steps.

    Outcome

    Only a ticket that needs a specialist decision goes on to step 3.

  3. Record the account and the charge

    Fill in the account ID, invoice number, charge amount and charge date on the ticket. Attach the invoice from the billing console, so the specialist has no need to ask again.

    Outcome

    The ticket carries everything a specialist needs to act on the charge.

  4. Check the account for a dispute or renewal

    Read the banners on the account in the billing console. A Dispute open banner, or a Renewal due banner with renewal within 30 days, changes the escalation.S3 For either banner, follow Exceptions and recovery before step 5.

    Outcome

    The agent knows whether the standard escalation or an exception applies.

  5. Escalate with the macro

    Apply the Escalate to billing macro and pick the reason code that matches the request. Write a three-line internal note: what the customer asked, what Tier 1 checked, and the decision needed.

    Outcome

    The ticket waits in the billing specialist queue under its reason code, with the Tier 1 agent as a follower.

  6. Reply to the customer

    Tell the customer that a billing specialist has the ticket, and give the SLA due time as the latest answer time. Promise no amount and no outcome.

    On a ticket with no earlier reply, this message is the first human reply. It stops the first response time clock.

    Outcome

    The customer knows who has the ticket and when the next answer is due.

  7. Follow the ticket to resolution

    Stay on the ticket as a follower until the specialist resolves it.1 If the customer writes again first, reply within the SLA due time and add the message to the internal note.

    Outcome

    The specialist resolves and closes the ticket, and the satisfaction survey goes out 24 hours later.S4

Part 04

Exceptions and recovery

The steps change in four cases: a large refund, a disputed charge, a renewal, and a returned or late escalation.

Refund above the agent limit

Each role approves refunds up to its own limit, per invoice and before tax.S1 A refund above the Tier 1 limit goes to a specialist with the reason code Refund approval.

Tier 1 agent
Up to $250
Billing specialist
Up to $2,500
Billing operations lead
Above $2,500, with finance sign-off

Never split a refund across invoices or tickets to bring it under a limit. Above $2,500, the reply in step 6 says that approval adds up to 2 business days.S1

Disputed charge

A charge the customer has disputed with the bank is out of Tier 1's hands. Issue no refund or credit, since the bank decides the outcome.

Escalate with the reason code Dispute, and the disputes desk among the billing specialists takes the ticket. A claim that a charge was never authorised follows the same path.

Customer in renewal

An account due to renew within 30 days escalates as in step 5, with two changes.S3 Add the account manager as a follower, and set the priority to High.

Change no plan, price or renewal date on the ticket. The account manager owns those decisions.

Returned or overdue escalation

A specialist returns an escalation tagged Needs info when it lacks what step 3 records. Add the missing details within 1 business day, then apply the macro again.S3

An escalation is overdue once it passes its SLA due time.2 Due times are 4 hours for chat and phone, and 24 business hours for email, web form and social.S5 On an overdue ticket, add the billing duty lead as a follower.
