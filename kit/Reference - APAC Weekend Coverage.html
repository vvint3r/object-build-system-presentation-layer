APAC weekend coverage

Three ways to answer APAC tickets that arrive at weekends, compared on customer wait and agent hours.

Part 01

Definition

APAC weekend coverage is the choice of who answers APAC tickets between the end of the Friday shift and the start of the Monday shift. Without cover, those tickets wait for Monday.

Part 02

The decision has one owner and a due date, and rests on a four-weekend pilot.

Part 03

Options

The options differ in who answers the APAC weekend queue.

Weekday only

APAC agents work Monday to Friday, and weekend tickets wait for Monday. It was APAC's only cover before the pilot.

Weekend shifts

Two APAC agents work each weekend shift, 08:00 to 20:00 local time, and most take a weekend day back on Wednesday, as in the pilot.S1

Americas overflow

Tier 1 Americas agents answer the APAC weekend queue during their own weekend shifts, with no APAC agent on duty.

Part 04

Criteria

Five criteria measure the wait a customer sees and the hours each option costs. The first two carry the decision, since the Monday queue drives APAC's slow first response.S3

Definitions
Monday first response
Median first response time for APAC tickets created on Mondays. A weekend backlog delays every Monday ticket that queues behind it.
Monday queue at open
Open APAC tickets waiting at 09:00 local time each Monday. It counts the work each weekend leaves for Monday.
Weekend first response
Median first response time for APAC tickets created on Saturdays and Sundays. It is the wait a weekend customer sees.
Wednesday first response
Median first response time for APAC tickets created on Wednesdays. It shows the cost of moving weekday hours to the weekend.
Weekend agent hours
Agent hours a week spent on the APAC weekend queue, whether moved from APAC weekdays or taken from the Americas rota.

Part 06

Recommendation

The recommended option is Weekend shifts, which the Q3 review and the pilot review also propose.S3S5

In the pilot, the Monday queue fell by more than half and weekend tickets got a first reply within 2 hours. The cost is a slower Wednesday, when most weekend agents take their day back. Americas overflow uses 28 fewer weekend hours a week, but leaves weekend customers waiting over 4 hours, and takes Americas agents off their own queue.

Part 07

Change conditions

The recommendation rests on the cost of weekend shifts and on weekend volume.

  • If the Q1 2027 budget does not fund 48 weekend hours a week, Americas overflow becomes the recommendation.
  • If APAC weekend tickets fall below 80 a day, against 126 in the pilot, Americas overflow answers them within 2 hours and becomes the recommendation.S2S4

Part 09

Examples

The example follows one illustrative weekend ticket under each option.

Saturday email

An email arrives at 10:00 on a Saturday, APAC time. Under Weekday only, the first reply comes at 09:30 on Monday, after 47h 30m. Under Weekend shifts it comes after 1h 20m, and under Americas overflow after 4h 30m, once an Americas agent reaches the queue.
