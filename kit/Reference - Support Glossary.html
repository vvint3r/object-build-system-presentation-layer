Part 01

Definition

The support operations glossary gives the shared sense of each service term in the support documents. Each term follows the policy or dataset it cites, and changes only when that source does. Plan, price and invoice terms fall outside it.

Part 02

Terms

Terms are grouped by what they describe, and run A to Z within each group.

Clocks and waits

Definitions
Business hours
08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays, in the time zone of the Tier 1 region that takes the ticket. Due times for email, web form and social count only these hours.S1
First response time
Time from a ticket's creation to the first reply a person sends, counting every hour. Automatic acknowledgements and internal notes do not stop it. Also called FRT.S2
Specialist queue wait
Time an escalated ticket waits from the escalation until a specialist picks it up. Like first response time, it counts every hour.S2

Due times and attainment

Definitions
Resolved within SLA
A ticket resolved before its SLA due time. A period's figure is the share of its resolved tickets that meet it. Also called SLA attainment.S1
SLA due time
The deadline for resolving a ticket under the service level agreement (SLA). It falls 4 hours after creation for chat and phone, and 24 business hours after for email, web form and social.S1

Queues and escalation

Definitions
Backlog
Tickets open or waiting on the customer at 23:59 UTC each Sunday, when the weekly snapshot is taken.S2
Escalation
Handing a ticket from Tier 1 to the billing or technical specialists, with an escalation macro, a reason code and an internal note. The escalating agent stays on as a follower.S3
Intent routing
A classifier that reads each new chat and sends it to the Tier 1 queue for billing, technical or account questions. Email and web-form tickets wait in one shared queue.S4
Reason code
The code on an escalation that names the decision a specialist must make, such as Refund approval or Dispute. It decides which specialist desk takes the ticket, such as the disputes desk for Dispute.S3
Tier 1
The first line of agents, in regional teams, who pick up every new ticket from the queues and either resolve it or escalate it.S4

Survey

Definitions
Customer satisfaction
The share of survey responses scoring 4 or 5 on a 5-point scale, with every response counted once. The survey goes out 24 hours after a ticket is resolved and closed. Also called CSAT.S5
Response rate
The share of resolved tickets whose survey gets a response. The lower it is, the less a period's customer satisfaction speaks for every ticket.S5

Part 04

Examples

Both examples set first response time beside the due time: one counts every hour, the other business hours only. The tickets and times are illustrative.

Disputed charge by email

A customer emails at 09:02 on a Wednesday about a disputed charge, and an automatic acknowledgement goes out at once. At 09:40 a Tier 1 agent escalates with the reason code Dispute, then replies. First response time is 38m, since the acknowledgement does not stop the clock.

The disputes desk picks the ticket up at 14:10, a specialist queue wait of 4h 30m. Counting 24 business hours from 09:02 on Wednesday puts the due time at 13:02 on Friday. Resolved at 16:00 on Thursday, the ticket counts as resolved within SLA.

Email over a weekend

An email arrives at 17:00 on a Friday, and the first reply comes at 10:00 on Monday. First response time is 65h, since that clock counts the whole weekend. Only 3 business hours pass, so 21 of the 24 remain before the due time.
