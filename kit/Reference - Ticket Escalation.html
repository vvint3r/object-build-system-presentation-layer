Ticket escalation

How a ticket that Tier 1 cannot resolve moves through the specialist queue and engineering to closure.

Part 01

Definition

Ticket escalation is the path that carries a ticket to the role able to resolve it. That role is Tier 1, the first line of agents, or a specialist. Engineering joins when a fix needs a code change, then hands the ticket back.

Part 02

Part 03

Flow

Work passes between three roles. Two decisions either close the ticket or send it on, and engineering returns a fixed ticket to the specialist.

Workflow

Ticket escalation

A ticket enters at intent triage and leaves when the role holding it closes it.

  1. Intent triage

    The routing classifier tags each chat with its intent, such as billing; an agent sets it on other channels.

    Actor
    Routing classifier or Tier 1 agent
    Hands on when
    The intent is set

  2. Tier 1 resolution

    An agent answers from macros and the help centre, and refunds up to $250 an invoice.S5

    Actor
    Tier 1 agent
    Hands on when
    The fix needs a specialist decision

  3. Specialist queue

    A billing or technical specialist decides the case and fixes what account access and configuration allow.

    Actor
    Billing or technical specialist
    Takes in
    Ticket with the account and the problem recorded
    Hands on when
    The fix needs a code change

  4. Engineering fix

    A product engineer repairs the data or ships the change, then returns the ticket to the specialist.

    Actor
    Product engineer
    Produces
    Fix note on the ticket
    Hands on when
    The fix is deployed; the ticket returns to Specialist queue
Exceptions
  • A charge disputed with the bank goes straight to the disputes specialists, with no refund at Tier 1.
  • A security or data-loss report skips Tier 1 and goes to the on-call product engineer.
  • A customer reply after close reopens the ticket at Tier 1 resolution.

Part 06

Examples

Each example follows one illustrative ticket through the stages.

Billing email escalated on a Tuesday

A billing email arrives at 11:20 on a Tuesday, and an agent sets its intent to Billing. The Tier 1 agent records the charge, escalates the ticket and replies to the customer at 12:05. A billing specialist corrects the invoice the next morning and closes the ticket.

Chat that needs a code change

A chat arrives tagged Technical by the classifier, and Tier 1 cannot fix the fault. A technical specialist finds a data error that only a code change can repair. Engineering ships the fix two days later, and the specialist confirms it with the customer and closes the ticket.
