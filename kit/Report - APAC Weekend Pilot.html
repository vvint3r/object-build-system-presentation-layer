Part 01

Executive summary

Two volunteer agents a shift on APAC weekends cut the Monday queue by more than half over four weekends. Monday first response fell 45 minutes, but weekend tickets were resolved no faster, and Wednesday first response slowed 27 minutes.S1

Summary

Key findings

  1. Weekend cover cut the Monday queue by more than half. Tickets waiting at Monday open fell from 212 to 96 on average across four Mondays.S1
  2. Monday first response fell 45 minutes. The median fell from 3h 12m to 2h 27m, and weekend tickets got a first reply in 1h 50m instead of 29h 40m.S1
  3. Weekend tickets were resolved no faster. Median resolution moved from 46h 10m to 44h 30m, because billing hand-offs still waited for Monday.S1
  4. Wednesday first response got 27 minutes slower. Volunteers took their rest days midweek, and the Wednesday median rose from 2h 24m to 2h 51m.S1S2
  5. Four weekends with no control region leave the size of the effect uncertain. Billing volume after the v4 launch and a macro rewrite also changed between the two periods.S3
Part 02

Situation

Until 16 September, APAC had no staffed weekend coverage, so tickets created from Friday evening waited for Monday.S3 Monday mornings opened with a queue of weekend tickets ahead of the day’s new ones.

APAC’s Q3 median first response was 3h 20m with weekends counted, against 2h 31m on weekdays alone.S3 The pilot set out to clear weekend tickets before Monday, and so shorten the Monday wait.

Part 03

Intervention

From 19 September, two volunteer agents covered each APAC weekend shift, 08:00 to 20:00 local time on Saturday and Sunday.S2 They worked no overtime and took their rest days midweek.

Timeline

Part 05

Confounds

In or near the two periods, three other changes could each move a measure without the pilot.

  • Billing v4 launched on 12 August, ten days before the first period, and billing volume was still settling.S3 A busier before period would overstate the queue’s fall.
  • The macro library rewrite of 26 August fell inside the before period.S3 If agents kept getting faster with the new macros, the after period gains from it.
  • APAC weekend volume fell 3%, from 1,040 to 1,010 tickets.S1 Fewer tickets alone would shrink the Monday queue a little.

Appendix A

Methodology

A.1

Terms used in this report
Definitions
Monday queue
APAC Tier 1 tickets waiting for an agent at 09:00 local time each Monday, averaged over the four Mondays of a period.
First response
Elapsed time from a ticket’s creation to the first human reply, as First response time defines it. Monday, Wednesday and weekend medians cover tickets created on those days, local time.
Weekend resolution
Median time from creation to solve for APAC tickets created on a Saturday or Sunday, whatever day they were solved.
No change
A move of less than 5% of the before value, in either direction. A move of 5% or more counts as better or worse.
A.2

Data sources and coverage
  • APAC Tier 1 tickets created 22 August to 14 September and 19 September to 12 October 2026, local time.
  • Spam and merged duplicates are left out, with the filters of the Q3 review.

Limitations

  • Four weekends each side
    Each median rests on four days a period, so one unusual Monday or weekend moves it.    OpenFix due 2026-12-14
  • No control region
    No other region ran without cover in the same weeks, so the season cannot be separated from the pilot.    By design
  • Volunteer rota
    Volunteers chose to work weekends, and a permanent rota may draw less experienced agents.    Open
