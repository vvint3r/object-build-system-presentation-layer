Part 01

Executive summary

Support Tooling plans to route email and web-form tickets by intent by 14 December, as chat has been since 8 July. Those two channels carry 55% of tickets and resolve fewer within SLA than chat does.S1

Email within SLA, Q3 88.3% 6.7 pp below target
Chat, Q3 96.1%
Web form within SLA, Q3 86.9% 8.1 pp below target
Chat, Q3 96.1%
Summary

Key findings

  1. Routing goes live in two steps in December. Email routes from 7 December and the web form from 14 December, after the November renewal cycle.S3
  2. A shadow gate stands before any live routing. The classifier’s labels must match the closing queue on 90% of two weeks of email.S3
  3. Live email routing waits on six new billing specialists. Routing sends billing email straight to the specialist queue, which already waits a median 9h 40m.S1
  4. Two decisions are due in early November. Contract cover for empty specialist seats by 2 November, and what a missed shadow gate moves by 6 November.
Part 02

Goal

By 14 December 2026, every email and web-form ticket lands in a billing, technical or account queue on arrival, as chats have since 8 July. The Q3 review expects email SLA attainment to rise about 4 points, still short of the 95% target.S1

Email and the web form carry 55% of tickets and resolved 88.3% and 86.9% within SLA in Q3, against chat’s 96.1%. The estimate rests on chat, whose first response fell 27% once routing went live.S1

Part 04

Out of scope

The roadmap changes how email and web-form tickets reach a queue; other channels and SLA terms stay as they are.

  • Social messages, 5% of tickets, stay in the shared queue until the January result is known.
  • Phone calls keep their menu routing, which already sorts callers by topic.
  • SLA due times stay at 24 business hours for email and web form, so the January result compares like with like.

Appendix A

Methodology

A.1

Terms used in this report
Definitions
Within SLA
A ticket resolved before its SLA due time. Email and web-form tickets have 24 business hours; chat has 4 hours around the clock.
Intent routing
Sending a ticket on arrival to the billing, technical or account queue, from a classifier’s reading of its text, rather than to one shared queue.
Shadow agreement
The share of labelled emails whose label matches the queue the ticket closed in. In shadow running the classifier labels without routing, so agents’ choices are the yardstick.
