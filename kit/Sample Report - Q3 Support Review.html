Part 01

Executive summary

Support handled 48,210 tickets in Q3, 7.4% more than Q2, while median first response fell 27% and the open backlog shrank 39%. Service-level attainment rose to 91.2%, below the 95% target. The shortfall lies in billing, where the August Billing v4 launch added sustained volume.

Summary

Key findings

  1. Chat intent routing cut first response time by 27%. Median first response fell from 3h 05m to 2h 14m after routing went live on 8 July, and chat resolves 96.1% of conversations within SLA.S1
  2. The backlog fell 39% despite higher volume. Open tickets dropped from 1,940 to 1,186, reaching the ≤ 1,200 target in the final week of the quarter.S1
  3. SLA attainment is 3.8 points short of target. Billing specialists resolved 82.6% of tickets within SLA, the lowest of any team, after Billing v4 raised billing contacts by 31%.S1
  4. Satisfaction reached 87.4%, 0.6 points below target. CSAT dipped during launch week and recovered to 89.5% by the end of September.S2
Part 02

Demand

Volume ran slightly above Q2 until the Billing v4 launch on 12 August pushed week 7 to 4,310 tickets, the quarter’s peak. It then settled near 3,650 a week, 3.5% above the same Q2 weeks. Q3 closed at 48,210 tickets, 7.4% more than Q2’s 44,870.

Note
Weekend volume is under half the weekday level, but APAC had no staffed weekend coverage until 16 September, so its Monday queues absorbed the difference.
Part 03

Speed & service levels

TL;DR Responses got 27% faster and the backlog fell under its ceiling, but email and the web form hold SLA attainment 3.8 points below target.

Median first response time fell to 2h 14m.1 Overall SLA attainment rose +1.6 pp to 91.2%, still short of the 95% target. Chat met SLA on 96.1% of conversations, while email and the web form stayed below 89%.

The backlog fell every week except launch week and closed the quarter at 1,186 open tickets, 39% below the 1,940 it opened with. For the first time this year, it sits under the backlog ceiling of 1,200.2

Warning
Email and the web form carry 55% of volume. Even if every other channel resolved 100% of tickets within SLA, overall attainment would reach only 93.3% until those two improve.
Questions
Why did the backlog rise in week 7?
Billing v4 launched on 12 August and billing contacts rose 31%. The backlog peaked at 1,710 that week and fell every week after.
Does SLA attainment include weekends?
For chat and phone, yes; both run around the clock. Email, web-form and social SLAs count business hours only.

Part 05

Customer sentiment

Across 10,317 survey responses, CSAT averaged 87.4%, 0.6 pp below the 88% target. It dipped to 85.6% in launch week, when billing questions doubled. It then climbed for six straight weeks to finish at 89.5%, above target for the final month.

Appendix A

Methodology

A.1

Terms used in this report
Definitions
First response time
Elapsed time from ticket creation to the first human reply. Auto-acknowledgements are excluded.
Within SLA
A ticket resolved before its SLA due time. Due times are 4 business hours for chat and phone, 24 business hours for email, web form and social.
Backlog
Tickets in open or pending state at the snapshot time. Tickets waiting on the customer count as pending.
CSAT
Share of survey responses scoring 4 or 5 on a 5-point scale, weighted by response count.
A.2

Data sources and coverage
  • All tickets created or resolved between 2026-07-01 00:00 and 2026-09-30 23:59 UTC.
  • Excludes 1,904 spam and merged duplicate tickets (3.8% of raw volume).
  • Q2 comparisons use the same filters, re-run on 2026-10-05.
A.3

Query: SLA attainment by team
sla_by_team.sqlSQL
-- share of tickets resolved inside their SLA window, by team
select
  team,
  count_if(resolved_at <= sla_due_at) as resolved_in_sla,
  count(*) as resolved_total,
  round(100 * resolved_in_sla / resolved_total, 1) as sla_pct
from support.tickets_fact
where resolved_at between '2026-07-01' and '2026-09-30'
  and is_spam = false
group by team
order by sla_pct desc
